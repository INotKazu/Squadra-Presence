# Squadra Presence and KazuCorp Brand Policy

Effective August 26, 2026

This policy protects users from confusing unofficial copies with genuine
Squadra Presence releases. It supplements the [personal-use license](LICENSE)
and does not grant permission to copy, modify, or redistribute the software.

## Project identity

The following identify Kazuma / KazuCorp and the official project
(collectively, the **Project Marks**):

- **Squadra Presence**
- **KazuCorp** and **KazuCorp Systems**
- **Kazuma's Picks** and its creator-curated presentation
- the Squadra Presence logo, application icon, original startup sequence,
  original chime, visual identity, and distinctive interface presentation
- the official GitHub repository and release channel at
  `INotKazu/Squadra-Presence`
- the project's Discord Rich Presence application identity and asset namespace

The Project Marks do not include Dragon Ball, DRAGON BALL GEKISHIN SQUADRA,
Discord, DBGS Builds, or material owned by their respective rights holders.

## Uses that do not require permission

You may:

- accurately name Squadra Presence when discussing, reviewing, or linking to
  the official project;
- share screenshots or videos of an unmodified official release for
  non-commercial commentary, guides, or personal social posts;
- state that a guide or community post works with Squadra Presence; and
- link people to the official repository or official release page.

These uses must be truthful and must not imply sponsorship, partnership,
endorsement, or official status.

## Uses requiring written permission

Without prior written permission from Kazuma / KazuCorp, you may not:

- name or brand another program, repository, website, account, or service
  **Squadra Presence**, **KazuCorp**, **KazuCorp Systems**, **Kazuma's Picks**, or
  anything confusingly similar;
- use the project logo, icon, startup identity, original chime, screenshots, or
  interface presentation to brand another product;
- rehost, mirror, bundle, repackage, or redistribute an installer, source
  archive, update artifact, or modified build;
- use the official Discord Application ID, Rich Presence art keys/assets,
  updater endpoint, signing identity, or GitHub release channel for another
  program;
- remove or replace creator credit, or claim that a fork or modified copy was
  made, approved, supported, or published by Kazuma / KazuCorp; or
- use the Project Marks in a way that is deceptive, harmful, unlawful, or likely
  to compromise a user's account, credentials, files, or device.

Permission to use a Project Mark does not grant permission to use third-party
Dragon Ball, Bandai Namco, Discord, or DBGS Builds material.

## Authorized modifications

If Kazuma / KazuCorp gives written permission for a modification or derivative
work, it must use a clearly different name, icon, Discord application identity,
and update channel unless that permission explicitly says otherwise. It must
also state prominently that it is unofficial and is not supported by Kazuma /
KazuCorp.

## Reporting impersonation

Suspected impersonation, copied releases, or misleading redistribution can be
reported through the official GitHub repository:

https://github.com/INotKazu/Squadra-Presence

Include the URL and screenshots of the suspected misuse. Do not post passwords,
private signing keys, access tokens, or other secrets in a public report.

## Changes

Kazuma / KazuCorp may update this policy as the project evolves. Existing uses
will be evaluated under the policy that was published when the use began unless
otherwise required by law.
